# Mission 14 — Behauptungen kritisch prüfen

- Source hour: Module 6, Stunde 1; the original activity remains at `pages/ki8/modul-6.html#stunde-1`.
- Public route: `pages/ki8/stunde-14/` (`/ki8/stunde-14/`).
- Duration: 45 minutes.
- Learning focus: examine who made a claim, which evidence supports it, how the outcome was measured, and whether the comparison is fair. Critical thinking means asking for support, not rejecting every claim automatically.
- Data and privacy: all examples are fictional. The mission does not request real student results; answers/progress remain local in the isolated `ki-14` browser key.
- Resources: The first phase links BR/so geht MEDIEN’s “Recherche – So klappt der Faktencheck” as a partial student-facing video. It is German and under 10 minutes according to Fakefrei; the BR lesson package includes a schedule, worksheet and quiz on source trust, the two-source principle and balanced reporting. It does not specifically teach study measurement methods or fair comparisons, and no reuse license is stated. As closer teacher support, Medien-Doktor’s Fakten-Doktor provides films, presentations and worksheets for assessing scientific experts and studies; it is intended for teachers from approximately grades 8/9. Film runtimes and reuse licenses are unstated. Both are external links. The fictional app claim, existing tasks, phase timing, and browser-local `ki-14` progress remain unchanged.
