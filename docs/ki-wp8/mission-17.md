# Mission 17 — Bias und Vorurteile untersuchen

- Source hour: Module 6, Stunde 4; the original activity remains at `pages/ki8/modul-6.html#stunde-4`.
- Public route: `pages/ki8/stunde-17/` (`/ki8/stunde-17/`).
- Duration: 45 minutes.
- Learning focus: inspect fictional training data for missing groups and situations; explain how limited data can bias predictions; do not infer fairness from overall accuracy alone or treat model output as a judgment about people.
- Data and privacy: all dataset cases are fictional; no student records or personal data are needed. Answers and progress remain local in the isolated `ki-17` browser key.
- Resources: The first phase links KI-Campus’s German video [“Biases beim Maschinellen Lernen: Welche Auswirkungen haben verzerrte Trainingsdaten?”](https://ki-campus.org/lernangebote/videos/biases-beim-maschinellen-lernen-welche-auswirkungen-haben-verzerrte). Medienkompetenzrahmen NRW lists it for grades 8–13 under CC BY-SA 4.0; no runtime is stated. The KI-Campus source page says it comes from “Data Literacy für die Grundschule,” a course for prospective primary teachers, so this audience context differs from the catalog grade listing. It introduces model training and balanced data but does not specifically teach group-level error metrics or why aggregate accuracy alone is insufficient; M17’s fictional tasks provide that practice. Optional teacher support: HubbS’s [“Verzerrungen und Repräsentativität (Aufgabe 1)”](https://hubbs.schule/mediathek/datenbeschaffung-und-aufbereitung-04-verzerrungen-und-repraesentativitaet-aufgabe-1) is German and CC BY 4.0, and covers representativeness/selection bias; age and runtime are unstated. The existing Experience AI lesson remains another optional candidate for ages 11–14, with its download license unverified. External links only; no material copied or embedded.
